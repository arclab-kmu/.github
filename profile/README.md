### 👋 Welcome to the **Autonomous Robotics & Control Lab** at Kookmin University

We design **novel control theories and high-performance actuation for intelligent robotics** — from robust control and reinforcement learning, to humanoid and manipulation platforms, to self-driving laboratories.

</div>

---

## 🔬 Research

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">🛡️<br>Robust Control</h3>
      <p align="center">Disturbance-observer (DOB) design in the H∞ framework, optimal &amp; dual DOB for nonlinear and slow–fast systems, DOB-based MPC, and Lyapunov-based reinforcement learning that closes the sim-to-real gap.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">🤖<br>Intelligent Robotics</h3>
      <p align="center">High-performance electro-hydraulic actuation, a compact Bernoulli gripper, vision-guided dual-arm manipulation, 3D mapping with 2D LiDAR, and pneumatic biped design &amp; walking control.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">🧪<br>Autonomous Laboratory</h3>
      <p align="center">Self-driving robotic labs for chemistry &amp; materials synthesis — quantum-dot thin-film process optimization, solvent formulation, and automated vial &amp; batch processing.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
